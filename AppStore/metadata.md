## en-US

### Promotional text

```
Paste the link from your TV provider. Kanal sorts out live TV, films, series and the guide on its own — and nobody else in the house has to learn how.
```

### Description

```
Kanal is an IPTV player for iPhone, iPad and Apple TV that the rest of your household can use without you in the room.

ONE FIELD TO SET UP
Paste the link your provider gave you. M3U, M3U8 or an Xtream login — Kanal works out which one it is, finds the programme guide, and sorts everything into live TV, films and series. There is no settings screen to get wrong afterwards.

SEARCH THAT FORGIVES
Type a film the way it is called where you live. If your provider listed it under another name, Kanal looks it up and finds it anyway. Search runs on your device, instantly, and would rather say "not found" than send you to the wrong film.

FILMS THAT ACTUALLY PLAY
Many providers deliver films in formats the built-in player cannot open. Kanal carries a second playback engine and switches to it on its own, behind the same controls. Subtitles, audio tracks, AirPlay and picture in picture included.

A REAL TV GUIDE
Channels down, hours across, now and next on every favourite. If a channel's stream drops, Kanal tries the next source for the same channel by itself.

FOR EVERYONE IN THE HOUSE
Profiles keep favourites and progress apart. A child's profile starts empty and shows only what a grown-up has allowed — chosen channels, an age limit, or single titles by name — protected by a four-digit code.

SET UP YOUR APPLE TV FROM YOUR PHONE
Your Apple TV shows a code; your iPhone sends the setup across the room over your own network. No typing passwords with a remote.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no Kanal server, no tracking, no ads. Your provider details stay on your devices and in your own iCloud Keychain.

FREE, AND KANAL PLUSS
Everything above works for free. Kanal Pluss removes the small watermark over the picture and the occasional reminder after watching. Monthly, yearly with a one-week free trial, or a one-time purchase.

IMPORTANT
Kanal is a player only. It includes no channels, films or other content, and is not affiliated with any provider. You need your own subscription or playlist, and the right to watch what you add.

Terms of Use: https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/
Privacy: https://kanalplayer.com/privacy
```

## Age rating questionnaire

Answer honestly for what the app can show, not what it ships with. Kanal
plays whatever the user's own source contains and cannot know what that is,
so App Review rates IPTV players as if the content were unrestricted.
Recommended answers:

- Every content category (violence, sexual content, profanity, horror, etc.):
  **None** — the app itself contains none of it.
- **Unrestricted web access: No.** There is no browser.
- **User-generated content: No.**
- **Parental controls: Yes** (child profiles behind a code).
- **Age assurance / messaging / gambling / contests: No.**

Expect App Review to raise the rating to 17+/18+ regardless, as it has done
for comparable players. Accept that rather than argue: it limits nothing for
the households Kanal is built for, and fighting it delays the release.

## App Privacy ("nutrition label")

**Data Not Collected.** Kanal runs no server and has no analytics or ads.
What leaves the device is either the user's own traffic to their own
provider, Apple's private iCloud (not accessible to the developer), or a bare
film title sent to Wikidata/TMDB with no identifier — none of which is
"collected" in Apple's definition. Matches `privacy.txt` in the app and
`Site/privacy.html`.

## Export compliance

`ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption` is already `false` in both Info.plists. The
only cryptography is Apple's own (HTTPS, Keychain, CryptoKit AES-GCM sealing
the user's own iCloud snapshot), which is exempt.

## In-app purchases

The three products (`com.bomann.kanal.pro.monthly`, `.yearly`, `.lifetime`)
are `READY_TO_SUBMIT`. On the first version they must be **selected on the
version page** ("In-App Purchases and Subscriptions") so they go to review
with the app; otherwise App Review cannot buy them and the paywall shows no
prices.

Each subscription also needs its own review screenshot (the paywall, on
iPhone) — take it from `AppStore/screenshots/`.

### Notes (paste as-is; under 4000 characters)

```
Kanal is a media player for IPTV services the user already subscribes to. It ships with no channels, films or other content, contains no links to any provider, and does not suggest where to obtain playlists. On first launch it asks for one link — the user's own M3U playlist or Xtream login.

To review the app, please use this test playlist. Every stream in it is either a public test stream (Apple's own BipBop sample, Akamai's and Mux's test streams) or a Blender Studio open movie released under Creative Commons, hosted by the Blender Foundation or the Internet Archive:

https://kanalplayer.com/demo.m3u

1. Launch Kanal, step through the short introduction, paste the address above into the single field and tap Connect.
2. Live TV: open the Live tab and play "Apple BipBop" or "Akamai Live Test".
3. Films and series: open Films and play "Tears of Steel" (Apple's player) or "Sintel" (Matroska, played by the bundled VLC engine — the controls are the same). Series holds "Caminandes", three episodes.
4. Profiles: Settings › Manage profiles › Add profile, and turn on "Profile for a child". Choose a four-digit code when asked. Switch to the child profile: it shows nothing until a grown-up allows a channel, an age limit or a title.
5. Kanal Pluss: in the free tier a small "KANAL FREE" mark sits over the video; tapping it, or Settings › Get Kanal Pluss, opens the purchase screen with the monthly, yearly (one-week free trial) and lifetime options. "Restore purchase" is on the same screen. All features remain usable without purchase; Pluss removes the watermark and the reminder shown after watching.
6. Apple TV: the same playlist can be entered with the remote, or sent from an iPhone via Settings › Send to Apple TV (scan the code the TV shows). Both devices must be on the same network.

The app uses the bundled VLC (LGPLv2.1, listed under Settings › Licences) only to decode Matroska/AVI files that AVFoundation cannot open. No code is downloaded or executed at runtime.

Kanal has no server and collects no data; the privacy policy is at https://kanalplayer.com/privacy.
```
