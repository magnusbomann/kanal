Copyright and DMCA

Last updated 23 August 2026

We take copyright seriously, and the most useful thing this page can do is be precise about what Kanal is, so that a notice reaches whoever can actually act on it.

Kanal hosts nothing

Kanal is software that plays media from an address the user types in. We operate no servers that store, cache, index, stream or link to content. There is no catalogue in the app, no search of anything but the user's own list, and no directory of providers anywhere on this site. We have no ability to see what any user has added, and no ability to remove it.

The consequence is straightforward: there is nothing here for us to take down. A notice about a stream must go to the service hosting that stream, or to its network provider, who can act where we cannot.

If you believe this site infringes

If material on this website itself — text, images, code — infringes a copyright you own or represent, tell us and we will act promptly. Send:

  • identification of the work you say is infringed;
  • the exact URL of the material you are asking us to remove;
  • your name, address, telephone number and email;
  • a statement that you believe in good faith the use is not authorised by the rights holder, its agent, or the law;
  • a statement, under penalty of perjury, that your notice is accurate and that you are the owner or authorised to act for the owner;
  • your physical or electronic signature.

Send it to copyright@kanalplayer.com.

Counter-notice

If we remove something of yours and you believe it was a mistake or a misidentification, you may send a counter-notice with the same contact details, identification of the removed material and where it appeared, and a statement under penalty of perjury to that effect.
