Support

Write to hello@kanalplayer.com and a person answers. Before you do, these fix most of what comes up.

Nothing appeared after I added my link

Check the address for a stray space at either end — links get wrapped when they are pasted out of an email. If your provider gave you a username, host and password rather than a link, enter the host on its own; Kanal will ask for the rest.

Live TV plays, films do not

This is the classic symptom of a player without a second engine, and Kanal should handle it by switching automatically. If a film still refuses, tell us the provider and we will look — the useful detail is whether the failure is instant or after a few seconds.

My guide is missing programmes

Provider guide files are very often malformed, and Kanal repairs what it can rather than giving up on the whole file. Settings will tell you how much of your channel list the guide actually covered. If the coverage is poor, the file itself is usually the cause, and your provider can supply a better one.

A film is filed under the wrong name, or has no poster

Provider naming is inventive. Search still finds most titles under the name they carry in your language. If something is identified wrongly in a child's profile, you can override that title directly.

Something is missing from a child's profile

That is deliberate: a child's profile shows only what has been allowed, so anything unrated or unclassified stays out until a grown-up allows it. Allow the title by name from the parent profile.

Still stuck

Tell us the device, the iOS or tvOS version, and what you did just before it went wrong. If it concerns a specific stream, do not send us your credentials — the part of the URL after the host is enough.
