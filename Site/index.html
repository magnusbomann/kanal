Questions

The honest answers.

Does Kanal come with channels?

No. Kanal arrives empty. It ships no channels, hosts nothing, resells nothing and has no catalogue of its own. It plays a subscription you already pay for, the way a music player plays files you already own.

How does search find a film under another name?

Every search runs against your own library first — offline, on the device, in about 12 ms on a 50 000-entry library. Only when that comes up short does Kanal ask what the words mean and look again under every other name the film carries. It never guesses: a near-enough match is refused rather than offered.

How do child profiles work?

A child’s profile starts empty and fills up only with what a grown-up has allowed: a whole section, a rating, or a single title or channel by name. Nothing outside the allowance can be reached — not by search, not by a link, not by resuming what was last watched — and your decisions survive your provider reissuing its links.

Is using Kanal legal?

The player is. What you point it at is your responsibility: you need the right to watch whatever you add. Kanal has no way of knowing what a given link contains, and takes no part in supplying it.

Which providers work?

Anything speaking standard M3U or M3U8, and Xtream panels. If your provider gave you a link, or a username, host and password, that is all Kanal needs.

Why can other apps not play my films?

Because most panels serve films and episodes as Matroska, and the player built into Apple’s systems cannot open it. Kanal carries a second engine for exactly this, and switches to it without telling you.

Do I need an account, or an internet connection to Kanal?

Neither. There is no Kanal account and no Kanal server. Your device talks to your provider, to Apple’s iCloud if you sync, and to public film databases for posters — never to us.

What does it cost?

Kanal is free to watch with, for as long as you like: live TV, films and series all play. Kanal Pluss removes the small watermark and the occasional reminder, and is available by the month, by the year with a free week to try it, or once for good. There is no advertising either way.

What languages does Kanal speak?

The interface ships in fourteen languages, from Norwegian and Swedish to Arabic and Brazilian Portuguese. Search understands far more than that — it looks for a film under the name it has in your language, whatever your provider called it.

Is my Apple TV supported?

Yes. iPhone, iPad and Apple TV share one app and one design, tuned for a remote where that matters.
