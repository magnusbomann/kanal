Open source

Last updated 23 August 2026

Kanal is built on work other people gave away. This page lists it, states the licence, and — where the licence gives you rights — tells you how to use them. The same list is inside the app, under Settings › Licences.

VLCKit

Kanal embeds MobileVLCKit and TVVLCKit from the VideoLAN project, which is what lets it play Matroska and the other formats Apple's own player refuses.

VLCKit is licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 2.1 or later. Under that licence:

  • The source is at code.videolan.org/videolan/VLCKit, and the released frameworks Kanal uses come from download.videolan.org.
  • We have made no modifications to VLCKit. Were we to make any, we would publish them.
  • You are entitled to relink Kanal against a modified version of the library for your own use. Write to legal@kanalplayer.com and we will provide what is needed to do so.

A copy of the LGPL v2.1 is included in the app.

Wikidata

Titles and translations come from Wikidata, whose data is released into the public domain under CC0 1.0. No attribution is required; we give it anyway, because a free, key-less, multilingual database of what things are called in every language is the reason Kanal's search works at all.

TMDB

Where cover artwork is shown, it may come from The Movie Database. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Everything else

The rest of Kanal — its interface, its parsing, its organisation of a catalogue, its search index — is our own code, and is not open source.
